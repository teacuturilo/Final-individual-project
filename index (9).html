Where the data comes from

    Decomposition times are estimates compiled by NOAA and Woods Hole Sea Grant. Real times depend on sunlight, water and temperature, and NOAA notes that plastic never fully disappears: it breaks into microplastics. Orb sizes are calculated from the listed durations, using the middle of each range. Data checked in November 2026.
